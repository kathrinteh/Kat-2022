Butterly

2021 | Branding / Pattern Design / Menu Design / Motion Design

Butterly is a donut shop that specializes in making custom donuts. The heart of what makes Butterly unique is their changing, transformative nature.

Customers are welcome to choose from a wide array of icings, toppings, and more, in order to form their perfect donut.
